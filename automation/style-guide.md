# HexaPixora content style guide

**Audience:** owners and managers of small and mid-sized businesses deciding on
web, AI, cloud and digital-transformation work. Not developers.

**Voice:** grounded, practical, anti-hype. Name trade-offs and limits honestly
("where it helps, where it does not"). Plain English, short paragraphs, no
buzzword stacks, no exclamation marks, no emojis. Never invent statistics,
quotes, clients or case studies — if a number matters, cite the source inline
with a link, otherwise leave it out.

**Topics (pillars):** digital transformation, AI automation for business,
web design & conversion, cloud & hosting, e-commerce growth, marketing growth.
Skip consumer news, gadget launches, crypto, politics, and anything we can't
connect to a decision a business owner makes.

## Post format

- 1,500–2,200 words of Markdown. **No H1** (the title is rendered separately).
- Open with 2–3 paragraphs that frame the reader's real problem, then `##` sections,
  `###` for sub-points. Include at least one table or checklist when it fits.
- End with a `## Final Thoughts` section, then a single soft call to action linking
  to `/contact-us` (e.g. "If you're weighing this for your business, talk to us.").
- 2–4 internal links to existing posts (`/insights/<slug>`) where genuinely relevant,
  plus at most one link to the matching service page: `/web-design-development`,
  `/app-development`, `/custom-software-platforms`, `/uiux-design`,
  `/branding-identity`, `/graphic-motion-design`, `/digital-marketing-seo`,
  `/social-media-management`, `/performance-advertising`.
- `faq`: 3–5 questions people actually search for, answered in 2–3 sentences each.

## Metadata

- `slug`: lowercase kebab-case, primary keyword first, ≤ 8 words.
- `metaTitle` ≤ 60 chars; `metaDescription` 140–160 chars, includes the keyword.
- `excerpt` ≤ 280 chars. `metaKeywords`: 4–6 comma-separated phrases.
- `categories`: 1–2 names from the live category list only.
- `tags`: 4–6 lowercase phrases.
